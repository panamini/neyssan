FAQ

Honest answers.

What is two weeks, exactly?

A private AI workspace for serious job applications. It stores the context that matters — your CV, the job, your voice and your proof — then helps you produce the right CV, cover letter and proposal.

What is the context layer?

The context layer is the structured memory behind the application: CV facts, job requirements, match analysis, tone, drafts, exports and readiness state. It is what makes the AI grounded instead of generic.

Do you reuse my data?

No. Your application data stays private. We do not sell it, and we do not reuse your CV, job descriptions or drafts as shared training data.

Where do Mistral and Stripe fit?

Mistral powers document understanding and extraction. Stripe handles trusted checkout. Neither changes the core rule: your application context remains private and under your control.

Why does taste matter if AI keeps getting better?

Because better AI makes generic writing abundant. Taste decides what deserves to be said, how it should be framed and when the document is finished.

Can agents use two weeks?

two weeks is designed to become agent-readable through structured context and MCP-ready surfaces. Agents can discover the right context, but humans keep review, permission and final accountability.
