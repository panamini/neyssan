FAQ

Honest answers.

Is two weeks an AI resume builder?

Yes. It helps you import, write, tailor and export resumes. The difference is that two weeks is built around private context: your CV, the job, your match and your proof.

Is it an ATS resume builder?

Yes. The resume templates use real text and clean structure so applicant tracking systems can parse your CV. The product also helps map resume keywords to the job description.

Does two weeks generate cover letters?

Yes. It works as a cover letter generator, but it does not start from an empty prompt. It uses your resume, the job offer, your voice and your strongest match evidence.

What does “private context layer” mean?

It means your job-search material has one authority layer: CVs, jobs, requirements, match evidence, drafts, proposals, exports and safe-send state. Agents can help because they know the context.

Do you reuse my data for training?

No. Your resume, job descriptions and application documents are private user context. They are not reused to train shared models.

Why mention Mistral and Stripe?

Mistral supports AI workflows such as parsing, matching and drafting. Stripe handles checkout. The product focus stays on private application context, document quality and accountable output.
