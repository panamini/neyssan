FAQ

Honest answers.

Is two weeks an ATS-friendly résumé builder?

Yes. Every CV template renders as real, parseable type — no images, no bitmap headings, no trick fonts. Your résumé passes Workday, Greenhouse, Lever and Taleo screens.

Can I import an existing PDF résumé?

Yes. Drop the file in. Mistral parses it into structured sections in seconds. Edit, restyle, export. No re-typing.

How is the AI cover letter generator different from ChatGPT?

two weeks holds your voice profile, your CV, and the job offer in the same context. The AI rewrites sentences, never your personality. The output reads like you, not like a corporate memo from a generic LLM.

What's a “job proposal,” exactly?

A one-page document you send to a hiring manager, a freelance prospect, or a referral. Sharper than a cover letter, lighter than a pitch deck. Best when you want to start a real conversation about a real role.

Is two weeks free?

Yes — start free, build your first CV, send your first cover letter. Pay only when you need extra exports or premium templates.

Why “two weeks”?

Two weeks is the time it takes to land an offer if your application is good — and the time most people lose when it isn't. We picked the deadline as the brand.
