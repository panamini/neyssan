The correct benchmark dimensions for TwoWeeks

Use these as your core evaluation categories whenever you review any screen, flow, marketing page, or feature.

A. Brand fit

Does the interface feel like TwoWeeks specifically, or like a random SaaS?

Score on:

distinctiveness
recognizability
coherence between landing, app, exports, and AI
emotional tone consistency
B. Conversion power

Can a cold visitor understand in under 5 seconds:

what this is
who it is for
what problem it fixes
why it is faster/better
what to do next
C. Trust and seriousness

Would a cautious user trust this for a job application or proposal?

Score on:

visual maturity
absence of gimmicks
legibility
copy precision
output credibility
D. Productivity ergonomics

Can a repeat user move faster every session?

Score on:

action discoverability
editing flow
shortcut potential
state predictability
low context switching
E. AI usability

Does AI reduce work, or create more work?

Score on:

suggestion quality
controllability
traceability
rewrite usefulness
low “AI cleanup tax”
F. Output quality

Do exported documents look employable, premium, and human?

Score on:

typography
spacing
hierarchy
professionalism
ATS-safe variants
print realism
G. Universality

Can this work for:

one-shot urgent users
long-term job seekers
freelancers
consultants
less design-literate people
more ambitious aesthetic users
H. Retention design

Does the product deserve repeat use?

Score on:

saved workspace usefulness
document system
versioning
reuse
proposal pipeline
long-term account value
I. Growth and shareability

Does the product naturally generate:

exports
referrals
links
visible outcomes
portfolio-worthy artifacts
J. Defensive moat

What becomes hard to copy?

Score on:

brand system
better import normalization
document intelligence
export quality
workflow ergonomics
trust from repeated success
3. The benchmark set I would actually use

Not 20 random references.
Use a focused benchmark stack.

Tier 1 — product operating system benchmark

These are your behavior benchmarks.

Linear

Benchmark for:

density without chaos
disciplined spacing
confidence in minimal UI
strong tool identity
clean dark surfaces

What to borrow:

restraint
crisp control bars
low-noise interface
high signal-to-noise ratio

What not to borrow:

too much abstraction for mainstream users
Vercel

Benchmark for:

premium engineering calm
monochrome confidence
marketing clarity
brand-to-product continuity

What to borrow:

confidence in restraint
sharp typography
premium neutrals
clear CTAs

What not to borrow:

being too technical/cold for job seekers
Notion

Benchmark for:

modular editing mental model
progressive complexity
approachable power
inline controls + floating actions

What to borrow:

contextual editing
predictable structure
AI actions near content

What not to borrow:

too much blankness / weak hierarchy
Tier 2 — outcome/productivity benchmark

These are your workflow benchmarks.

Canva

Benchmark for:

template browsing behavior
novice comfort
broad-market conversion

Borrow only:

familiarity of output browsing
approachable onboarding
preview-first confidence

Do not borrow:

visual clutter
template overload
generic creator-feeling
Craft / Raycast

Benchmark for:

intimate tool feeling
polished micro-UX
premium, quiet interface

Borrow:

precision
delight through finish, not noise
state clarity
Apple

Benchmark for:

reduction
message discipline
perceived product quality
“this feels done”

Borrow:

fewer stronger decisions
typographic discipline
purposeful hierarchy

Do not borrow:

over-sanitized emptiness that hides function
Tier 3 — category benchmark

These are the category enemies / comparators.

ResumeLab

Benchmark for:

funnel conversion
strong hand-holding
obvious section editing
reassurance for anxious users

But you should beat it on:

taste
output quality
long-term utility
AI usefulness
proposal expansion
product dignity
generic AI resume tools

Benchmark only as anti-patterns:

too much AI copy sludge
too much glow
too much magic language
too many generic suggestions

quieter
sharper
more believable
more reusable
