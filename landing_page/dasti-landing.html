FAQ

Short answers for long-tail SEO and real buying questions.

What is an AI resume builder?

An AI resume builder helps you write or improve resume sections. two-weeks focuses on job-specific resume versions, not generic resume decoration.

How do I match my resume to a job description?

Paste the job description, scan your resume, compare keywords and requirements, then rewrite bullets that prove the missing skills with real experience.

Can two-weeks generate cover letters?

Yes. It generates a tailored cover letter from your resume and the job description so the letter matches the role without sounding copied.

Does two-weeks replace a job application tracker?

It includes tracking, so resumes, cover letters, saved jobs, application status, and follow-ups stay connected.
